# Product Development Checklist

A lean product design pipeline for Token's hiring tracker, ending at wireframes.

## Stages

1. **Product Framing Note** — Define what we are building, for whom, and the exact scope boundaries.
2. **Core Workflow Outline** — Define the main user journeys and candidate stage progression.
3. **Information Model Note** — Define the core entities, fields, statuses, and relationships needed to support the workflows.
4. **Screen Map** — Define the screens required for the MVP, their purpose, and how they connect.
5. **Low-Fidelity Wireframes** — Sketch the structure and layout of the key MVP screens.
