# Current Hiring Process Notes

## Overview
The current hiring process at Token is manual, email-driven, and prone to errors. It starts when the hiring manager shortlists candidates from LinkedIn and ends when HR issues offers to approved candidates, potentially moving to waitlisted candidates if needed.

## Current Process

### 1. Candidate Shortlisting
- The hiring manager shortlists candidates from LinkedIn.
- The hiring manager copies the LinkedIn profile links of shortlisted candidates.
- The hiring manager sends these links by email to the HR person responsible for the hiring project.
- The hiring manager asks HR to schedule department interviews.

### 2. HR Outreach for Department Interviews
HR contacts candidates to arrange interviews.

Possible outcomes at this stage:
- Candidate is contacted successfully and an interview is scheduled.
- HR tries to contact the candidate by phone but cannot reach them.
- Candidate says they are no longer interested in the position.

Additional notes:
- A candidate may also withdraw before the interview is conducted.
- The hiring manager may need to reschedule an interview due to an emergency.
- Because of company image considerations, interviews are not cancelled outright.

### 3. Department Interview by Hiring Manager
- The hiring manager conducts the interview.
- The hiring manager takes notes during the interview.

Possible decisions after or during the interview:
- Candidate is clearly unsuitable and is rejected directly.
- Candidate is unclear / undecided and is placed in an undecided pool.
- Candidate performs well and is moved directly to the next step.

Additional notes:
- As more interviews are completed, the hiring manager may later revisit undecided candidates.
- Undecided candidates may later be moved either to rejected or to the next stage.
- Because there is no platform to manage this, the hiring manager often waits for interviews to accumulate and then sends batch emails for the next step.

### 4. HR Interview Stage
- The next stage after department interviews is the HR interview.
- HR conducts interviews with candidates and takes notes.
- HR later gives feedback to the hiring manager.
- HR also tends to handle these in batches and replies in batches after multiple interviews.

Possible outcomes during HR interview outreach:
- Candidate cannot be reached.
- Candidate withdraws from the process.
- Candidate is contacted and the interview is scheduled.

Additional notes:
- In parallel, HR conducts interviews with the candidates whose interviews have been scheduled.
- After those interviews are completed, HR shares notes and feedback with the hiring manager.

### 5. Offer Approval and Offer Handling
- Based on HR feedback, the hiring manager approves candidates for offers.
- The hiring manager may approve multiple candidates for offers.
- HR then proceeds one by one, starting from the top candidates.

### 6. Waitlist Handling
- The hiring manager may place candidates on a waitlist.
- Waitlisted candidates do not automatically receive offers.
- If main candidates do not accept offers, HR asks the hiring manager whether to move forward with other candidates.
- The decision to move a waitlisted candidate forward or reject them is made by the hiring manager, not HR.

Possible next decisions for waitlisted candidates:
- Approve HR to issue an offer.
- Reject the candidate.

## Main Problems Observed
- The process is manual.
- Communication is handled through email.
- Information is distributed across people rather than stored in a shared system.
- Process steps are often handled in batches rather than continuously.
- The workflow is prone to errors and status confusion.
- Notes and decisions may be delayed or fragmented.
- Candidate state changes (unreachable, withdrawn, rescheduled, undecided, waitlisted, approved) are not managed in one place.

## Goal of Improvement
Create a product that improves this workflow by providing a clearer, more reliable way to track shortlisted candidates from post-shortlist interview handling through offer acceptance decisions.
